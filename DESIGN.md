# Design: Granite Coast Supply

**Purpose.** A catalog for shop buyers, and a private desk for the work behind it. The catalog persuades by showing the pieces clearly and naming every specimen. The desk is a tool: dense, plain, fast on a phone.

**Audience.** Owners and buyers at gift shops, rock shops, museum stores and oddity shops.

**The one idea.** Each photo is cut to the outline of a glass dome and set on a dark turned base, with a collection label under it: the name, the scientific name in italics, the origin. Everything else stays quiet.

**Palette.** Felt `#f4f6f5` (ground), card `#ffffff`, spruce `#14282b` (ink, bases, the desk bar), slate `#55666a` (secondary text), line `#d3dbd9`, glass `#e7eeec` (empty domes). Morpho blue `#1b56c9` is the only accent: links and focus. Brass `#8a6417` for warnings, moss `#1f7a4d` for cleared, rust `#b3261e` for stopped.

**Type.** Instrument Serif for headings and scientific names (italic, as a naturalist writes them). IBM Plex Sans for everything else. Both served from this site.

**Never.** Prices on the public page, buttons on the public page, labels in capitals above headings, card grids with icons, gradient text, stock photos, a dark "oddities" look with skulls. The pieces carry the mood.

**Motion.** None.
